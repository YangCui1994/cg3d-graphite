## Method

Per probed step the driver captures, per fluid node: the three-stage
colour sums in device arithmetic (equilibrium `eq` -> post-recolor `rc`
-> post-correction `fin`; f64 sums of stored f32 values), the local
colour-gradient magnitude `cc`, pre-step `rho_r/rho_b/psi`, the full
post-correction populations `g_r/g_b` (19), and the post-scatter
accumulators `rhor/rhob`.  A host f64 gather replicates the production
scatter exactly (verified: a standalone Taichi replay of the scatter
reproduces `rhor` to 2.4e-7 = f32 atomic-ordering noise; the global
permutation identity `sum(incoming) == sum(sent)` holds to 3.6e-12).
Node classes: `cc>0` / `cc==0` (exact), `mixed` / `pure` via
`chi = min(rho_r,rho_b)/(rho_r+rho_b)` with primary threshold 1e-6
(sensitivity 1e-7 / 1e-5 in `class_stats.csv`), `wall_adj` = any solid
among the 19 streaming targets.

Key identities (f64, per step):

    R_r(node)  = fin_r - rho_r_pre                     (local closure)
    Req_r      = eq_r - rho_r_pre                      (equilibrium stage)
    dacc_r(ip) = rhor(ip) - gather_r(ip)               (f32 accumulate
                                                        rounding at the
                                                        receiving node)
    Delta M_colour = sum_nodes R + sum_ip dacc         (verified below)

Runs: `dx_C1_T3C1s` / `dx_C3_T3C1s` (pre 1500, probe 40),
`ac_C1_T3C1s_20k` (contract C), `bg_C3_T3C1s` (pre 20000, probe 200).

## Finding 1 — the periodic-C1 one-sided local residual is the
uncorrected equilibrium-construction leak at NON-FROZEN cc==0 nodes

`dx_C1_T3C1s` (64x24x24 periodic two-phase slab, T3+C1/A0):

- `cc>0` nodes (17.6% of node-steps): local closure R_r mean
  -7.9e-12, frac_pos 0.542 — **closed and unbiased by scoped-C1**.
- `cc==0` nodes (82.4%): R_r mean **+4.66e-9**, frac_pos 0.785,
  identical to Req_r (the C1 increment Rc1 is exactly 0 there — the
  scope condition `cc>0` never fires).  This is the external-review B2
  residual.
- x-profile: far-bulk pure nodes (|x - interface| > ~6) are bit-frozen
  (Req_r ~ 1e-19, net_r ~ 1e-14).  The biased cc==0 nodes form a band
  of NON-FROZEN pure nodes adjacent to the diffuse interface
  (x ~ 34-40: Req_r = R_r ~ +1.0e-8/step, ongoing net mass motion).
- The `mixed` class (chi > 1e-6, 9.1%) is dominated by cc>0 nodes and
  is unbiased.  **The biased class is pure (chi ~ 1e-12), not mixed** —
  a chi-threshold scope extension would miss it; the operative
  discriminator is non-frozenness, detectable as dr != 0.

## Finding 2 — the periodic-C1 local bias ACCUMULATES (contract C)

`ac_C1_T3C1s_20k` (T3+C1/A0, 20000 steps, every 200):

    Mc slope = +8.85e-10/step (R2 0.927, frac_pos 0.778)
    second half (10k-20k): +8.08e-10/step (R2 0.965, frac_pos 0.780)

Not a plateau, not a transient: a persistent one-sided accumulation
(magnitude inside 2e-9, sign systematic).  Per contract E the selected
candidate must improve it >= 10x.

## Finding 3 — the C3 one-sided global drift is dominated by the f32
scatter-accumulate rounding, partially CANCELLED by the local residuals

`bg_C3_T3C1s` (T3+C1/A0, t=20000, 200 probed steps).  The budget
identity closes to f64 noise:

    dMr/step = -1.2825e-05 = sumR_r (+6.99e-06) + sumdacc_r (-1.982e-05)
    dMb/step = -2.688e-06  = sumR_b (+1.30e-05) + sumdacc_b (-1.570e-05)
    identity residual: 7.7e-12 / 2.3e-12

Per class (red, per node per step): the `cc>0` interface class carries
`sum_dacc_r` mean -7.18e-10 with frac_pos 0.494 — a ~0.6% sign
asymmetry of the f32 accumulate rounding at non-frozen nodes; its local
closure is unbiased (-5.5e-11).  The `cc==0` wall-adjacent pure class
carries the positive uncorrected leak (+4.4e-9/node at frac_pos 0.40,
mean-dominated) but only 6.3% of nodes.  Far bulk is bit-frozen
(dacc == 0 exactly).  This reproduces and explains the committed 60k
colour slope -6.43e-10/step: **two mechanisms of opposite sign
(positive local leak at cc==0 nodes, negative accumulate rounding at
cc>0 non-frozen nodes), partially cancelling.**

## Consequence for candidate design

- A closure-only scope extension (fix the cc==0 local leak) removes the
  positive term and leaves the accumulate term -> periodic-C1 slope
  would go NEGATIVE (~-1.6e-9/step from the dx window means), failing
  the >=10x improvement requirement.
- An accumulate-precision fix alone (f64 colour transport) removes the
  negative term and leaves the local leak -> periodic-C1 slope would go
  POSITIVE ~+3.8e-9/step, exceeding 2e-9.
- The gates therefore require BOTH interventions (candidates C1X + A1
  compared individually and combined in CANDIDATE_COMPARISON.md).

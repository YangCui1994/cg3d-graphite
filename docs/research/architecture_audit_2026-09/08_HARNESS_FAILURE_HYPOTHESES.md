## Proposed scientific-development structure

A compact extension can preserve the current executor/reviewer mechanics:

1. **Problem contract:** physical objective, assumptions, required observables and excluded claims; owner signs off on modeling choices.
2. **Formulation ledger:** primary equation/table → specialization → code symbol → invariant; implementation variants separate from arithmetic overlays.
3. **Benchmark admission:** derive expected relation, density/force/BC premises, topology and resolution, measurable signal, estimator and gate before freezing.
4. **Frozen execution:** bind source, instrument, geometry, gate, environment and artifact schema; publish both negative and positive evidence.
5. **Two review questions:** “Was this implemented/measured correctly?” and “Would this evidence decide the intended scientific claim?” A fresh context alone does not guarantee independent premises.
6. **Reframe checkpoint:** repeated failed prediction, changed theory/gate, or new physical scope returns to the problem contract. Do not keep relabeling stages without accounting for the parent research question.
7. **Promotion:** model validity, backend fidelity and application readiness are distinct decisions; keep immutable baselines for module ablation.

## Alternatives for the future reviewer to compare

| Option | Benefit | Cost / risk | Selection criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Add admission/claim ledgers to current harness | smallest change, reuses working provenance and isolation | depends on disciplined human review of premises | prefer if most failures are missing checks rather than runtime limitations |
| B. Separate research planning, numerical validation and production engineering lanes | makes changes of question/model explicit | more contracts and handoffs | prefer if scope changes recur despite lightweight admission gates |
| C. Rebuild orchestration around a full evidence graph | automatic invalidation and dependency queries | substantial infrastructure work may displace science | justify only with recurring cross-branch/provenance burden quantified from real cases |

**INFERENCE:** A is the lowest-cost starting experiment, not an architecture decision for the owner. Evaluate on one subsequent authorized stage: fewer invalid benchmark runs, traceable premise changes, no lost negative evidence, and lower reviewer reconstruction effort. Do not evaluate success by a higher PASS fraction. No configuration or automation change is made here.

## Jurin premise, explicitly reconstructed

**FACT from code:** `init_psi` defaults to total rho=1 in both phases, equilibrium uses the unit-density alpha, and `solver.step` adds `rho * force[a]` to momentum, without a liquid-only mask. `pass06.py` supplies `fz=-g_acc` and compares to a finite classical rise.

**INFERENCE under the standard two-phase hydrostatic assumptions:** capillary restoring pressure involves `(rho_liquid-rho_gas)*g*Delta h`, plus boundary/interface pressure terms. With equal phase density and equal acceleration, the weightless-gas approximation using only `rho_liquid*g` is not justified. Closed, disconnected gas pockets add pressure constraints; simply substituting a new denominator is not a complete benchmark redesign.

The later reviewer supplies hydrostatic measurements and rejects the published pinning inference. Their numbers are **SOURCE CLAIMS**, while the code-level premise mismatch is directly inspectable. Preserve the topology repair as useful work. Do not infer that changing to an open gas boundary alone automatically restores all classical assumptions; density, force and pressure conditions must be derived together.

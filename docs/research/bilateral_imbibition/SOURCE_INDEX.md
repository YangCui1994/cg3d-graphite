## Source Coverage Gaps

Searched for but **not available locally, or not covering the topic**:

1. **No source anywhere in the corpus simulates bilateral (opposing-front) spontaneous imbibition in a closed system.** Verified by keyword search (Washburn/collision/coalesc/front/entrapment families) across all 11 literature sources above. The nearest misses are S-WANG (closed periodic, but spatially uniform fronts), S-JEON (mid-stack inlet, but diverging fronts and open outlet), and S-LIU p. 34 (blob trapping behind a single advancing front).
2. **No source quantifies the pressure of an enclosed trapped-gas pocket under capillary compression** in an LBM simulation. S-WANG has a compressible gas EOS but never reports cavity pressure; S-WANNER / S-HLBM / S-SHODIEV vent the gas.
3. **Krüger 2017 does not cover the colour-gradient model family** (ch. 9 treats only Shan–Chen and free-energy; pp. 348, 418–419) **and contains no Lucas–Washburn / capillary-filling benchmark discussion** ("Washburn", "Lucas", "capillary filling/rise": zero full-text hits). Dynamic contact-angle theory (Cox–Voinov–Hoffman, hysteresis) is likewise absent.
4. **Not downloadable / paywalled** (recorded for completeness, from the corpus READMEs): Rothman & Keller 1988; Gunstensen et al. 1991; Latva-Kokko & Rothman 2005 (the recolouring form this solver uses); Huang et al. 2007 (MCMP contact-angle calibration); Huang, Sukop & Lu 2015 monograph; Akai et al. 2018 (CG wetting boundary condition). Their absence means CG wetting-BC theory is evidenced here only through S-LECLAIRE-PRE and reviews.
5. **No dynamic-contact-angle or contact-line-pinning study specific to the wall-colour (geometric) wetting mechanism of this solver family** was found locally; pinning evidence is generic recolouring-pinning (S-LIU, S-ZAHID).
